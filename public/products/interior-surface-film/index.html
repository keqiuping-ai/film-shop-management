Interior Surface Film questions

What surfaces can be wrapped?

Suitability depends on substrate stability, smoothness, cleanliness, shape, edge condition, moisture, and expected use.

Can surface film replace damaged material?

Film changes the finish but does not repair an unstable or severely damaged substrate. Repairs may be required first.

Is professional installation recommended?

Yes. Corners, seams, edges, heat exposure, and surface preparation have a major effect on durability and appearance.

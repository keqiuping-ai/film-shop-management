Yacht Protection Film questions

Can automotive PPF be used on a yacht?

Some film constructions may suit selected marine surfaces, but compatibility and exposure conditions must be reviewed for each project.

Does film work on gelcoat?

It may be suitable for compatible, stable, properly prepared gelcoat. Test and confirm the specific surface before full installation.

What areas are commonly considered?

Projects may focus on high-contact zones, selected painted panels, gloss surfaces, boarding areas, or compatible interior finishes.

Architectural Window Film questions

Can architectural film be installed on any glass?

Compatibility depends on glass type, coating, thickness, condition, exposure, and the selected film. A professional glass review is recommended.

Does privacy film work at night?

Reflective daytime privacy depends on lighting conditions. Frosted or opaque decorative films provide a different privacy effect.

Is safety film the same as impact-resistant glass?

No. Film is one component of a glazing system and should not be represented as a replacement for rated impact-resistant glass unless the complete tested system supports that claim.

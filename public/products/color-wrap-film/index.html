Color Wrap Film questions

What is automotive color wrap film?

It is a removable film applied over compatible vehicle surfaces to change color or finish without conventional repainting.

How should I choose a color?

Compare the physical sample, finish, product code, and real-world lighting. Screen colors are useful for browsing but are not a final color guarantee.

How much film does a vehicle need?

Required material depends on vehicle dimensions, coverage, panel complexity, installer technique, and allowance for rework.

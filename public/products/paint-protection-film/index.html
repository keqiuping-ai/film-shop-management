Paint Protection Film questions

What is paint protection film used for?

PPF is applied to compatible painted surfaces to help protect against everyday road wear, light scratches, and surface contamination.

Can I choose different finishes?

Available selections may include gloss, matte, satin, or specialty effects. Confirm the current catalog and inventory before ordering.

Does PPF require professional installation?

Professional installation is recommended because surface preparation, stretching, edge work, and contamination control affect the finished result.
